# Flame & Bun

Flame & Bun is a modern burger restaurant concept created to offer fast, satisfying meals with a fresh and premium fast-casual experience. The project presents the brand through a visually engaging landing page that highlights the menu, ingredients, and ordering experience.

## Business idea
The business idea behind Flame & Bun is to provide customers with high-quality burgers, sides, drinks, and sauces at an affordable price without compromising on taste or convenience. The brand is designed for people who want a quick, flavorful meal that feels fresh, satisfying, and worth coming back for.

## Target audience
Flame & Bun is aimed at:
- Students and young professionals looking for quick lunch and dinner options
- Families wanting easy, tasty meals for busy days
- Local food lovers who value quality, speed, and convenience
- Customers seeking a modern burger experience with bold flavor and good value
